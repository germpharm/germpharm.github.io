Lebanon Farmers Market

Katie Boardman spent years baking for school and church events. In 2022 she took a booth at the Lebanon Farmers Market. Neighbors came back every week. That community is what made a shop feel possible.

March 2023

2 West Park Street

Casey found the space on the pedestrian mall — formerly Twice on Sundae, next to Salt Hill Pub. Katie signed the lease. The shop opened March 2023. Owner: Katie Boardman. All are welcome.
